Who We Are
ONE OF THEM GROUP (Pty) Ltd is a Cape Town-based South African company established to develop and
coordinate a portfolio of practical service businesses. The group's business journey began in 2022 and it
was formally incorporated in 2026. Its current operating businesses are Cape Tree Masters and Fruitful
Bough.
Our focus is to build a sustainable operating structure, strengthen our service businesses and pursue
suitable opportunities with private clients, contractors, property managers, developers and procurement
organisations.
Our Approach
 Understand the client's scope, location, programme and site conditions before an engagement.
 Match each enquiry to the relevant operating business and available resources.
 Work within applicable permissions, safety requirements and agreed terms.
 Build long-term working relationships through clear communication and practical service delivery.
